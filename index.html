What Civitas is

More than ownership.

Civitas brings together a select group of people to invest in sport.

We combine disciplined investing with active partnership, operating expertise and The Collective, a carefully curated group of members.

We focus on clubs and franchises that are undercapitalised, under-managed and undervalued, but rich in heritage, community and growth potential.

Our ambition is to build more valuable sporting organisations while creating enduring relationships, extraordinary experiences and value that extends beyond the investment itself.

Our Approach

Sport is different.

Sporting assets combine scarcity, community, emotion and global attention in ways few traditional assets can replicate.

Investment philosophy

We invest where focused capital and hands-on involvement can change the trajectory of a club. These are the conditions we look for.

Mid-market focus

Sporting organisations where professionalisation and focused capital can materially influence enterprise value.

Active ownership

Influence and involvement rather than passive capital.

Incremental investment

Build conviction over time rather than necessarily acquiring control on day one.

Long-term capital

Build enduring assets rather than optimise for a short-term exit.

Multiple value levers

Sporting performance, commercial growth, talent, property and data.

Flexible and informed

Intelligence flow and strategic visibility across the global sports investment landscape are critical to our approach.

Enabling active management of long-term assets while keeping the flexibility to capture market opportunities.

Value-creation pillars

One connected system, joined by data

01

Community

A club's value starts with the people who care about it. We strengthen the bond between club, supporters and the wider community, because loyalty and local relevance underpin everything else a club can build.

02

Talent

We help clubs find, develop and create pathways for talent on the pitch and in the boardroom. Strong academies, clear recruitment and capable executives compound over time.

03

Real estate

Stadiums, training grounds and surrounding land are often a club's most underused assets. Our partners bring decades of development and operating experience to unlock their potential.

04

Data

Data connects the other three. We use data and technology to improve sporting, commercial and investment decisions, from player recruitment to how we select the next club to back.

The Civitas story

Why we started.

01 The observation

Civitas began with a simple observation. Many clubs outside the top tier have loyal supporters, meaningful assets and real potential, but lack the capital, expertise and professional structure to realise it.

02 The conviction

Our founders saw sport as an asset class unlike any other, and believed that combining investment discipline with hands-on operating capability could change what those clubs become.

03 The model

Adding The Collective, a strong group of members each bringing their own experience and relationships, turned that idea into a model: disciplined investing, active partnership and The Collective, working together.

Founders

Co-founder

Costa Alexiou

Costa has more than 23 years of international experience as an investor, operator and board director across investment, technology, real estate and global sport.

He is CEO of PMY Capital, having previously spent almost a decade as CEO of a US-based investment firm. His board experience spans professional sport and international businesses across technology, infrastructure and global professional services.

At Civitas, Costa focuses on investment strategy, capital allocation and governance. He works closely with portfolio leadership to help shape strategy, improve performance and create long-term value.

Co-founder

Ben Hautt

Ben has more than 20 years of experience as an investor and operator across real estate development, construction and alternative investments.

He is Managing Partner of Robles Partners, a US-based real estate development and investment firm.

At Civitas, Ben focuses on sourcing and evaluating investment opportunities and portfolio development. He works alongside management teams to strengthen operations, execute strategic priorities and build enduring value.
